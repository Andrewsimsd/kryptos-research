cipher fixtures, and [milestone 3](milestone-3.md) compiles the pinned `gt.c` and

from `k4-testy.c` under an explicitly seeded permutation null. Its raw counts and
uncertainty replace rounded reciprocal rates for local interpretation; the
historical selection of the tests remains a limitation.

Unknown bounds are unknown, not unlimited coverage.

Reproduction priorities are to validate the ACA example, reproduce `gt.c` with a separately derived constraint implementation, then reproduce fixed-width statistics with recorded seeds and raw counts. Broader width selection requires its own null procedure. No sampled or heuristic negative result in this table is an impossibility proof.

The Gillogly message and Dunin complete transcript independently yield the same four physical K4 fragments, lengths 4/31/31/31, exactly matching the plan's 97 letters. These are two published transcript sources, not two new inspections of the copper. Their copying histories are not established as independent. The reference file makes this limitation explicit.

Morse readings and sculpture placement remain attributed context with orientation and transcription uncertainties; they are not K4 key material.
