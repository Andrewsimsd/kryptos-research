# Research-source review for future K4 work

Reviewed 18 September 2026. This note records what was actually accessible; it
does not modify the frozen evidence ledger used by completed experiments.

| Source | Access and useful constraint |
| --- | --- |
| [Dunin's Kryptos collection](https://www.elonka.com/kryptos/) and [transcript](https://www.elonka.com/kryptos/transcript.html) | Read. Useful for clue chronology, aligned known letters, inscription layout, and image leads. Physical letter readings and layout hypotheses still require image-level confirmation. |
| [Gillogly's 1999 account](https://www.elonka.com/kryptos/mirrors/cypherpunks/1999/0930.html) | Read. Firsthand K1–K3 recovery context and keys; later K2 correction must be applied separately. |
| [K2 correction](https://www.elonka.com/kryptos/CorrectedK2Announcement.html) | Read. Keeps the corrected plaintext and as-inscribed version distinct. Its anomaly does not automatically transfer to K4. |
| [ACA Gromark specification](https://www.cryptogram.org/downloads/aca.info/ciphers/Gromark.pdf) | Read. Supplies the construction definition and known-answer control used by the project. |
| [Bean, HistoCrypt 2021](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/153/109) | Read. Motivates reproducible statistics and recurrence experiments. Reproducing a published method is validation, not a new cryptanalytic discovery. |
| [Sanborn's August 2025 letter](https://www.elonka.com/kryptos/OpenLetterAug2025.html) | Read. Context about secrecy and the continuing riddle; it is not an added plaintext crib. |
| [Scientific American, November 2025 presentation](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/cia-kryptos-puzzle-creator-releases-final-clues/) | Read. Firsthand event reporting attributes Egypt, the Berlin Wall, the World Clock, and a delivery theme to Sanborn. The linked original letter scan returned 403 and its exact wording was not reviewed. |
| [Berlin World Clock city record](https://www.berlin.de/sehenswuerdigkeiten/3561749-3558930-weltzeituhr.html) and [maker's description](https://kunsch-metallbau.de/referenzen-/weltzeituhr-berlin-alexanderplatz/) | Read. Support 24 sectors and a rotating 1–24 hour ring. Modern city labels include later changes; a 1989 hypothesis needs a period-correct label set. |
| Carter excavation notebooks at the Griffith Institute | Retrieval failed during this review. Any running-key milestone must freeze an identified edition and page/line extraction before searching. |
| Paradigm Kryptos pages | Direct retrieval failed. Do not infer present checker terms, availability, or current disclosure status from search snippets. |

Contextual clues motivate finite rules; they do not add known plaintext letters.
The future roadmap treats unavailable documents as evidence gates and keeps
modern reconstructions separate from period-correct data.
